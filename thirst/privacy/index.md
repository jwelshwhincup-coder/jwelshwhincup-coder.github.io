# Thirst: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Thirst: Water Tracker & Widget ("Thirst", "the app") is made by an independent developer. The short version: **Thirst does not collect your data.**

## What the app stores, and where
- **Your water log and settings** (glasses per day, daily goal, reminder times, toggles) are stored **only on your iPhone**, in storage the app shares with its widgets. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
- **Your free week** is the free "7-Day Trial" in-app purchase. Apple keeps the record of that purchase, so reinstalling the app doesn't restart the week.

## Reminders
Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push servers are involved.

## Purchases
Thirst Lifetime and the 7-Day Trial are handled by **Apple** through the App Store. We use **RevenueCat** to check whether you've bought Thirst Lifetime. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt from Apple; it does not receive your name or email. See <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy> and Apple's policy at <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## What we don't do
- No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
- No accounts and no sign-in.
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
- No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

## Children
Thirst is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Delete the app to remove your water log and settings. To take back a glass logged by mistake, hold + and choose Remove last glass.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
