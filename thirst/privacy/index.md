# Thirst: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Thirst: Water Tracker & Widget ("Thirst", "the app") is made by an independent developer. The short version: **your water log and settings never leave your iPhone.** The only data that does is what's needed to check your purchase, handled by RevenueCat, and none of it identifies you or is used for tracking or advertising.

## What the app stores, and where
- **Your water log and settings** (glasses per day, daily goal, reminder times, toggles) are stored **only on your iPhone**, in storage the app shares with its widgets. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
- **Your plan** (Free, or Thirst Plus Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime, and when a subscription or trial ends) is cached on your iPhone so the app and widgets know what's unlocked. The record of the purchase itself is kept by Apple.

## Reminders
Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push servers are involved.

## Purchases
Thirst is free to use. **Thirst Plus** (Monthly, Yearly, with a 1-week free trial on Yearly for eligible Apple Accounts, or a one-time Lifetime purchase) is sold by **Apple** through the App Store, which handles payment, renewals and cancellation. We never see your payment details. We use **RevenueCat** to check which plan you have and whether you can have the free trial. To do that, RevenueCat receives:
- **Purchase history**: your purchase receipt from Apple.
- **A user ID**: an anonymous app user ID RevenueCat creates. There's no account, and it is not your name, email or Apple ID.
- **A device ID**: the device's identifier for vendor, a random ID Apple gives each app developer on your device. It is not the advertising identifier.

These are used to unlock what you bought and, in aggregate, for purchase reports (for example, how many people subscribe to Thirst Plus). They are not linked to your identity, not used for tracking and not used for advertising. See <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy> and Apple's policy at <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## What we don't do
- No advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. RevenueCat's purchase reports, above, are the only analytics.
- No accounts and no sign-in.
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
- No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

## Children
Thirst is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Deleting your data
Delete the app to remove your water log and settings. To take back a glass logged by mistake, hold + and choose Remove last glass. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription: cancel in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. To have the purchase data held by RevenueCat deleted, email us (below).
