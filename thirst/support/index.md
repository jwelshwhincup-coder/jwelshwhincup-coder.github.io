# Thirst support

Email **jwelshwhincup@gmail.com**. We usually reply within two working days.

## Free and Thirst Plus

**What's free?** Logging water with the +, Today, your daily goal, glass size and units, your streak and this week on History, and one reminder a day (at 13:00, not once you've reached your goal). Free never runs out, and nothing you log is ever deleted.

**What does Thirst Plus add?** Smart reminders that pace your day and stop at your goal, your full History with the month calendar and heat map, the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, the Log Water control in Control Centre, and other amounts or an earlier time from the hold-+ menu.

**How much is Thirst Plus?** Monthly £1.99, Yearly £9.99, or Lifetime £19.99 (one payment, yours for good). These are UK prices; the App Store shows the price in your own currency before you buy.

**Is there a free trial?** If your Apple Account hasn't had one before, Yearly can start with 1 week free. It's offered when you close the plans screen. After 7 days it renews at the yearly price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. If you cancel, Thirst goes back to free and keeps everything you logged.

## Managing your plan

**How do I cancel?** On your iPhone open **Settings › [your name] (Apple Account) › Subscriptions › Thirst**, then **Cancel Subscription**. You can also get there from Thirst: **Settings › Manage Subscription**. Thirst Plus keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for, then Thirst goes back to free. Nothing is deleted.

**I reinstalled or got a new iPhone.** Open Thirst › **Settings › Restore Purchase** (also under the Continue button on the plans screen). Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime all come back with the same Apple Account. Your water log is stored on the device and isn't restored.

**I was charged and Thirst still says Free.** Tap Restore Purchase. If that doesn't help, email us with the date of the charge.

## Using Thirst

**How do I log a glass?** Tap the + on Today. Hold + for other amounts, an earlier time (up to 7 days back), or to remove the last glass. With Thirst Plus you can also log from a Home Screen widget or the Log Water control.

**Reminders aren't arriving.** Check iOS Settings › Notifications › Thirst, then Thirst › Settings › Reminders. Reminders stop once you hit your goal. Without Thirst Plus you get one reminder a day, at 13:00.

**My widget shows a lock.** Widgets are part of Thirst Plus. Tap the widget to see the plans; after you subscribe it unlocks by itself.

## Privacy
See the privacy policy: your water log stays on your iPhone.
