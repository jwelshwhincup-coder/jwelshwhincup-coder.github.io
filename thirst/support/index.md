# Thirst support

Email **jwelshwhincup@gmail.com**. We usually reply within two working days.

## Common questions
**How do I log a glass?** Tap the + on Today, the + on a Home Screen widget, or the Add glass control in Control Center or on your Lock Screen. Hold + for other amounts, an earlier time (up to 7 days back), or to remove the last glass.

**How does the free week work?** Tap "Start my free week" after setup. It's a free in-app purchase called "7-Day Trial", so nothing is charged and nothing renews. After 7 days, History stays visible and logging pauses until you unlock Thirst Lifetime, a one-time purchase.

**I reinstalled or got a new iPhone.** Open Settings in Thirst and tap Restore purchase. Thirst Lifetime and your free week come back with the same Apple ID. Your water log is stored on the device and isn't restored.

**Reminders aren't arriving.** Check iOS Settings › Notifications › Thirst, then Thirst › Settings › Reminders. Reminders stop once you hit your goal and after the free week ends without Thirst Lifetime.

**Widgets say "Thirst Lifetime".** Your free week has ended; tap the widget to unlock.
